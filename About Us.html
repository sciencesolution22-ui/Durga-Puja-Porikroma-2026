About Us

Welcome to Durga Puja Porikroma 2026!

This webpage has been created with enthusiasm and creativity by the students of our school to celebrate the spirit, traditions, art, and cultural heritage of Durga Puja.

Through Durga Puja Porikroma 2026, we invite you to explore the festive celebrations, beautifully decorated puja pandals, traditional rituals, cultural programmes, artistic creations, and the vibrant atmosphere that makes Durga Puja so special.

Our aim is not only to showcase the grandeur of this beloved festival but also to help students learn about our rich cultural heritage and appreciate the values of unity, creativity, devotion, community, and togetherness.

This website is a small effort by young minds to document and share the colourful journey of Durga Puja with everyone. We hope that this virtual porikroma brings the joy and excitement of the festival to your screen and inspires everyone to celebrate our culture with pride and respect.

Come, join us on this virtual Porikroma and experience the colours, creativity, traditions, and spirit of Durga Puja 2026!
